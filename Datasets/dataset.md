## Dataset

The datasets used in this project are available on Google Drive. They include the ISOT news dataset and prepared FNC-1 datasets for headline–article analysis and binary classification.

- **`ISOT_DS.csv`** – Contains news headlines (`title`), article text (`text`), subject, date, and label.
- **`FNC1_binary.csv`** – Contains prepared binary-labelled FNC-1 data.
- **`FNC1_merged.csv`** – Contains merged FNC-1 data for further processing and analysis.
- **`FNC1_binary_analysis.csv`** – Supports analysis and inspection of the prepared binary dataset.

The datasets are intended for preprocessing, headline–article comparison, and evaluation of TF-IDF and sentence embedding approaches. Label mappings should be verified before using the data for headline-consistency classification.
